# Cash rate path and mortgage pressure

The Reserve Bank cash rate target was 4.85 percent on 2026-11-05. It rose 5 times in 2026, from 3.60 percent before the first rise. The 2026-09-30 move to 4.60 percent shows up in the target and the overnight rate; the published change column for that day is blank. Mortgage repayments sit outside the grocery price index. Households can face both a higher cash rate and uneven grocery prices.
