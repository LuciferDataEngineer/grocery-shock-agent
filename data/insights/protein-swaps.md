# Beef, pork, poultry and eggs

Beef was up 5.8% in September 2026, after a 12.6% pace in July, so it is cooling. Pork was down 1.3%. Poultry was up 0.4%. Lamb was up 3.0%. Fish was up 1.7%. Eggs were down 2.3%. A household looking for a cheaper protein than beef can look at pork, poultry, and fish on price alone. Eggs are the relief line in the same shop. This is a price signal, not diet advice.
